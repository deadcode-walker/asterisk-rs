# Execution plans

Use a checked-in ExecPlan for work that is complex, risky, multi-session, or depends on discoveries.
Small work uses an in-session plan.

Every ExecPlan is self-contained and remains usable by a newcomer with only the current tree. It
must maintain: Purpose, Progress with timestamps, Surprises and Discoveries, Decision Log, Context
and Orientation, Plan of Work, Concrete Steps, Validation and Acceptance, Idempotence and Recovery,
and Outcomes and Retrospective.

Acceptance describes observable behavior and exact evidence. Update the plan at every stopping
point and move it from `active/` to `completed/` only when the outcome is proved.
